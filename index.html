Operator Precedence & Associativity

Operator Operation Precedence Associativity
^ Exponentiation 3 (Highest) Right-to-Left
* / % Multiplication / Division 2 (Medium) Left-to-Right
+ - Addition / Subtraction 1 (Lowest) Left-to-Right

Infix → Postfix: Scans Left-to-Right. Operands go directly to output; operators pop higher/equal precedence operators (strictly higher for right-associative ^).

Infix → Prefix: Reverses the infix expression (swapping ( and )), runs modified Shunting-yard (handling associativity in reverse), and reverses the final output.

Postfix Evaluation: Scans Left-to-Right. Pushes numbers; on operator, pops B then A and pushes A op B.

Prefix Evaluation: Scans Right-to-Left. Pushes numbers; on operator, pops A then B and pushes A op B.
